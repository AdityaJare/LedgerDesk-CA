Where practice operations break

Your firm runs on deadlines, documents, and review. All three break daily.

These are not edge cases. They are the default operating condition for most CA firms handling more than a few clients.

Deadline management

Every return type has a different calendar, and none of them sync.

GST, TDS, advance tax, ROC, audit timelines, notice responses — each tracked in a separate portal, sheet, or someone's memory. One missed date can cascade into penalties across the cycle.

Document collection

Staff spend more time chasing documents than reviewing them.

Purchase registers arrive late, bank statements are sent in parts, invoices come over WhatsApp. There is no shared record of what was asked, what was received, and what is still missing before filing.

Reconciliation

Mismatch work lives in spreadsheets that only one person understands.

GSTR-2A vs books, TDS as per Form 26AS vs ledger, bank vs cash book — each reconciliation is an export-download-compare cycle with no visibility into what was resolved or why.

Drafting & notices

Every notice reply and submission is drafted from scratch.

Associates spend hours writing replies to similar GST and income tax notices without reusable templates, past drafts, or linked source documents. Review comments get lost in email threads.

Partner review & sign-off

Partners review in WhatsApp screenshots and email forwards.

There is no structured queue showing what is waiting for review, what was returned with comments, and what was approved. Evidence of review and sign-off is scattered or absent.

Firm governance

Built for firms that handle sensitive client records.

Access, isolation, review logs, and evidence retention are not add-ons — they are the default operating layer.

Role-based access control

Partners, managers, executives, and clients each see only what their role permits. Assign access at the client, module, or engagement level.

Client-wise data isolation

Every client's documents, exceptions, drafts, and review history are logically separated. Team members access only assigned clients.

Review and sign-off logs

Every reviewer action — approval, return, comment, escalation — is timestamped and attributed. Sign-off trails survive staff turnover.

Document version history

Every upload, edit, and replacement is versioned. Previous versions remain accessible. No file is silently overwritten.

Exportable working paper packs

Export client-wise or engagement-wise evidence packs with all linked documents, review comments, and sign-off records in one structured archive.

Activity and change audit trail

Every action across the workspace — assignment changes, document access, status updates — is logged. The trail is immutable and exportable.
